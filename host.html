Incrementalism Guide

📌 Incremental bills make small changes. They face less opposition, pass more easily, but have limited immediate impact.
📌 Sweeping reforms make large changes. They face more opposition, may be gutted by compromise, but can have large impact if passed intact.
📌 The bill type is revealed after the vote. Listen to arguments — can you tell which it is?
